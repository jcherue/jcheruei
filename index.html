About

J Cheruei is a gospel rap artist from Monrovia, Liberia.

This isn’t just music. It’s a movement for the Kingdom. Every freestyle is a verse, and every bar is a message.

My mission is to use music, testimony, storytelling, and street evangelism to point people toward Jesus Christ.
